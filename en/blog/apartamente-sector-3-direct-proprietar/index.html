What "direct from owner" actually means in Sector 3

The market looks like this: on Imobiliare.ro, you will find 6,181 listings for sale in Sector 3, and about 15% of them are marked "direct from owner" or "no commission". Another 412 listings appear on OLX with the same filter. This is not a niche. It is a substantial slice of the market.

The supply concentrates in a few areas. Titan leads by far, with 321 listings on Imobiliare.ro and another 156 on OLX. Dristor comes next, followed by Theodor Pallady and Mihai Bravu. Buildings constructed after 2000 sell for 1,950 to 2,300 euros per square meter for two-room units, while older ones fall below this threshold.

What the label does not tell you: "direct from owner" simply means you do not pay an agency fee. Everything else remains the same. The paperwork still needs verification, the price still needs negotiation, and the notary still takes their share.

How much do you really save?

Take a 100,000 euro apartment and do the math with me. The agency fee would have been 2-4%, so you start right away with 2,000 to 4,000 euros left in your pocket. In direct negotiations, you can lower the price by another 5-10%, since you are speaking with the person selling rather than an intermediary caught between two commissions.

However, not all of those savings stay with you. Notary fees range from 0.5-1% of the transaction value, and the land registry extract costs another 20-50 lei. A modest amount, yes. But buyers using an agency usually get these checks included, whereas you must handle or pay for them yourself.

You still come out ahead, and by a good margin. If you plan your mortgage smartly, the difference easily covers the living room furniture. We explain how to calculate your monthly installment and down payment in detail in our mortgage guide.

Where you can get burned

Here comes the part listings do not advertise. An individual private seller does not automatically owe you a warranty for hidden defects. A bathroom leak, patched-up electrical wiring, dampness concealed with a fresh coat of paint: you discover these after moving in, and the legal road to restitution is long and goes through court.

Protecting yourself is straightforward, but you have to take the initiative. Request a land registry extract from OCPI to see in black and white whether the apartment has mortgages or pending litigation. Add a warranty clause for hidden defects in the contract, for at least one year. And only sign the notarized contract before a notary, who provides an extra layer of verification.

Sounds like bureaucracy? It truly is. Yet it protects you from the nightmare of losing your down payment on an apartment with tangled paperwork. A reputable seller will not shy away from any of these checks. Anyone who resists is already telling you everything you need to know.

Which neighborhoods in Sector 3 to look at

It depends on what you want and what monthly payment you can afford. Titan sits around 2,100 euros per square meter and offers the M1 metro line, IOR and Titan parks, plus schools and hospitals within walking distance. Dristor drops to around 1,950 and places you between two metro lines, with Sun Plaza right across the way.

Looking for something more affordable? Theodor Pallady stands at roughly 1,850, with quick access to Autostrada Soarelui, while Mihai Bravu rounds out the list at around 1,750 euros per square meter, also close to the metro. We keep prices by area and apartment type up to date on our pricing page.

The steps, from listing to keys

Here is what the entire journey looks like. Order the land registry extract from OCPI for 20-50 lei. Sign the pre-sale agreement with a down payment of 10-20% of the price. The notary verifies mortgages, litigation, and document authenticity. Sign the authenticated final deed and pay the full balance. Registration in the land registry under your name completes the process, also at OCPI.

Five steps, nothing complicated. Skipping any of them is entirely at your own risk, and here the risk is measured in tens of thousands of euros, not loose change.

Frequently asked questions

Do I pay any commission if the apartment is sold directly by the owner?
No. The 2-4% commission applies only when the transaction goes through an agency. However, notary fees of 0.5-1% of the price still apply, usually split between buyer and seller.
Do I get a warranty for hidden defects if the seller is an individual?
Not automatically. An individual seller has no statutory warranty obligation like a developer does. Ask for a warranty clause in the contract for at least one year, and inspect the apartment thoroughly before signing.
What should I check before signing with an owner?
The land registry extract from OCPI to verify mortgages and legal disputes. Then finalize everything through a notary with an authenticated contract. Without these two steps, you are buying on trust, and trust cannot be registered in the land registry.

Three things to remember

  1. 01

    The savings are real, but they are not free. You keep 2-4% of the price plus your direct negotiation gains, but in return you handle all the verification work an agency would have done.

  2. 02

    Paperwork trumps price, every single time. The OCPI extract and the notarized contract are not optional, because hidden defects and mortgages cannot be spotted during a casual viewing.

  3. 03

    The neighborhood dictates your budget. Between Mihai Bravu at 1,750 euros per square meter and Titan at 2,100, the difference for a 60-meter apartment exceeds 20,000 euros.
