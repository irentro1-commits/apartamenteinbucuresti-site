What the price of a new apartment actually means

Two 2-room flats can show a 30,000 euro price difference and actually cost the exact same. The difference lies in what is included in the total. In reputable new developments, the advertised price usually includes finishes, heating unit, and flooring. In others, you only get bare walls and basic utilities, with the rest billed separately. Out of your own pocket.

A simple question that saves you headaches: does this price include everything I need to move in, or do I need to set aside extra money for flooring, heating unit, interior doors, and other missing essentials? When comparing two listings, compare identical items. Otherwise, you compare apples to oranges and wonder why the final cost is so high.

At Ilioara Residence, for example, the price starting from 107,500 EUR without VAT includes full underfloor heating, 7-chamber Salamander triple-glazed windows, and an Ariston 24 kW heating unit. These are not asterisked add-ons, they are included in the price you see. Truly.

Why prices vary from unit to unit in the same building

In a building with 35 apartments, not all cost the same. Far from it. The difference comes down to three factors.

The floor. Lower-floor units are usually cheaper. Upper-floor units, offering better views and more natural light, cost more. At Ilioara, the lower floors sold first, and the remaining units are on floors 6 and 7, right where the view is at its best.

Surface area. Two rooms can mean 47 square meters or 65. Layout matters as well: a modular apartment that you can adapt to your lifestyle holds more value than one with rigid walls that do not suit you.

Orientation and hallway position. A unit facing the inner courtyard and another facing the street are not identical, even with the same room count. Ask about orientation before looking at the price.

New build versus old build: what you actually gain

A 2-room apartment in an old building in Bucharest may be 15,000 to 20,000 euros cheaper to buy. It seems like a deal. Then come the hidden costs: thermal insulation, rewiring and plumbing replaced piece by piece, 40-year-old pipes, and drafty windows.

In a new building you pay more upfront, but you receive modern insulation, new installations, high-performance windows, and an individual boiler to control your consumption. Five years down the line, utility savings cover part of that price difference.

The number of neighbors on your landing also matters. A short hallway with four units per floor means less foot traffic, more quiet, and fewer building management headaches. It is not a luxury detail. It is something that defines your everyday life.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a 2-room apartment in Bucharest cost in 2026?
In a new development in District 3, prices start at 107,500 EUR without VAT and reach 136,500 EUR without VAT, depending on floor and surface area. With 21% VAT, the actual total starts at around 130,000 euros. It is a notable investment, but you get a fully finished home.
What does the price of a new apartment actually include?
It depends on the developer. At Ilioara Residence, the price includes underfloor heating, Salamander triple glazing, and an Ariston boiler. In other projects, these may be paid extras. Ask for clear written terms, not casual promises.
Is it better to buy in a new building or an older one?
An older building seems cheaper upfront, but it entails renovation costs, outdated plumbing and wiring, and heat loss. A new build costs more initially, but offers modern insulation, brand-new systems, and an individual boiler for full control. Over five years, the math pays off.
