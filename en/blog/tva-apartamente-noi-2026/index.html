The standard rate is 21%, and that is the rule, not the exception

Since 1 August 2025, the standard VAT rate in Romania is 21%, set by Law 141/2025. It applies to homes as well, like almost everything else bought with an invoice. If you see a price displayed on a new apartment with plus VAT next to it, the rate you are looking at in 2026 is, almost certainly, this one.

The reduced rate of 9% on homes has not disappeared entirely, it is just no longer an option you choose at signing. It has remained as a transitional regime, for people who already had a preliminary contract (antecontract) signed before a date that passed long ago. It is not an open loophole. It is a door closing, with the hour written on it.

The difference between the two rates is not cosmetic. On a sum in the hundreds of thousands of euros, these percentages move as much money as a car, and the move shows up everywhere: in the deposit, in the sum the bank approves for you and in the final figure in the deed.

Who still catches the 9% rate and on what conditions

The conditions of the transitional regime are cumulative, meaning they all apply at once. If a single one is missing, the reduced rate no longer applies, however well you stand on the others.

  • The preliminary contract, that is the legal act concluded between living persons, must have been signed by 31 July 2025.
  • The usable area of the home must be a maximum of 120 sqm, excluding annexes.
  • The value of the home must be a maximum of 600,000 lei, excluding VAT.
  • It must be a single home bought at the reduced rate in that period.
  • The buyer must not have acquired another home with reduced VAT since 1 January 2023.

The last condition is not settled with a declaration and a smile. Public notaries are required to check, through an official register, whether you have bought a home at the reduced rate before. It is an official check, not a formality ticked off from a declaration, so it is worth knowing where you stand from the start.

All these conditions are read together with the deadline for completing the transaction. The deadline is the part that made the most noise this summer and it is also the part that decides everything for a buyer today.

The deadline moved to 30 September 2026, with a nuance that counts

The date by which the transaction had to be completed in order to catch the 9% rate was 31 July 2026. It was extended to 30 September 2026 inclusive. The Senate adopted the extension on 28 July 2026, and the Chamber of Deputies, the decision making chamber, on 29 July 2026.

The reason given publicly was the administrative blockage caused by the cyber attack on ANCPI. In short, people who had a complete file and the money ready could not finish the paperwork for reasons that had nothing to do with them. The extension was the answer to that blockage, not a relaxing of the rule.

We write the nuance because it is a matter of accuracy: on 3 August 2026, the date we checked the information in this article, promulgation and publication in the Official Gazette were not confirmed. The decision had passed both chambers, the final step of the procedure did not yet appear confirmed publicly. Anyone with a file on the table right in this period has to follow the current status, because an exact date in the calendar hangs on it.

How to read a price displayed plus VAT correctly

The displayed price is not the price you pay. It is the basis of calculation. At Ilioara, the 2 room apartments are between 107,500 and 136,500 EUR plus VAT, and the 3 room ones between 151,000 and 168,000 EUR plus VAT. A single parking space is 20,000 EUR plus VAT, a Klauss double parking space 25,000 EUR plus VAT, and the reservation 2,000 EUR plus VAT.

One single calculation, so you see the mechanism on figures. On an apartment of 107,500 EUR, the rate of 21% takes the sum payable to 130,075 EUR. The same arithmetic applies to any other item in the list, including the parking and the reservation, because they are all displayed the same way.

The other figures of the purchase start from here as well. The minimum deposit is 30%, and the question worth asking in writing, before the reservation, is which sum it is calculated on. The same percentage, applied to two different bases, gives you two different sums. It is the same with the bank: a simulation run on the displayed price gives you an instalment that does not exist, so start from the final sum when you look at the mortgage.

VAT is the biggest line added on top of the displayed price, but it is not the only one. Notary fees, the land registry entry, the bank file and furnishing all come on top, each at its own moment. We put them end to end in the article on the real costs of a new apartment, so you see the whole budget, not just the first figure.

What to do, concretely, before you sign

The first thing is to ask for the price in both forms, in writing: the displayed figure and the figure with VAT included. A serious seller sends it straight away, because they have the same figure in their own calculation. If the answer is late or vague, you have already learned something about how the work will go from here on.

The second is to ask directly for the estimated completion and handover date, not just the year. The rate is decided on the calendar, and the calendar is the only element in the whole discussion that is not negotiable. A whole year is too wide a margin when the rate hangs on one particular day.

The third is about who is selling to you. At Ilioara the sale is direct, from the developer, so you pay no buyer's commission and you put the VAT questions to exactly the person who has the answer. Of the project's 33 apartments, 12 are still available, so the discussion about the rate is held on a particular apartment, not on a list. What that looks like in practice we set out in the article on buying directly from the developer.

Frequently asked questions

What VAT do you pay on a new apartment in 2026?
The standard VAT rate is 21%, in force since 1 August 2025 through Law 141/2025, and it applies to homes as well. The 9% rate has remained only as a transitional regime, for purchases that meet all the conditions set by law. In practice, most buyers in 2026 pay 21%.
Can I still buy an apartment with 9% VAT in 2026?
Only if you fall under the transitional regime, and its conditions all apply at once: a preliminary contract signed by 31 July 2025, a usable area of no more than 120 sqm excluding annexes, a value of no more than 600,000 lei excluding VAT, a single home bought at the reduced rate in that period and no other home bought with reduced VAT since 1 January 2023. If the preliminary contract is signed after 31 July 2025, the discussion stops there.
By when can a transaction with 9% VAT be completed?
The deadline was 31 July 2026 and it was extended to 30 September 2026 inclusive, through a decision adopted by the Senate on 28 July 2026 and by the Chamber of Deputies, the decision making chamber, on 29 July 2026. On 3 August 2026, the date of the check, promulgation and publication in the Official Gazette were not confirmed. Anyone depending on this deadline has to follow the current status.
Can the 9% VAT be caught at Ilioara Residence?
It cannot. The project is completed in December 2026, so delivery falls after 30 September 2026 and no purchase made now fits the transitional regime. At Ilioara you pay 21%, and the prices on the site are displayed without VAT, so the rate is added on top of them.
