How to Get a Mortgage for an Off-Plan Apartment in 2026

Found your dream apartment, but it is still just a concrete skeleton? Don't worry. A mortgage for an off-plan apartment opens the door to a new home, even if the developer has only just poured the foundation. However, between fluctuating IRCC rates and the requirements of the Nordis Law, things can feel more complicated than they should be. That is not entirely true. If you know the steps, you can move fast and avoid the pitfalls that catch many buyers off guard. Let's look at how things stand in 2026.

What Changes in 2026: The Nordis Law and Your Protection

Law 207/2025, also known as the Nordis Law, took effect in March 2026. And it changed everything. Developers can no longer take your money without real safeguards. The reservation fee? A maximum of 5% of the total price, deducted directly from the final invoice. If they exceed this cap, the contract is null and void. Period.

Down payments? They go into a dedicated escrow account in the developer's name, tied strictly to your project. Not a single cent moves without proper authorization. Furthermore, the notary must record the preliminary sales agreement in the Land Registry on the very same day, or by the next business day at the latest. Otherwise, your contract risks being left in legal limbo.

IRCC in 2026: What Monthly Payments to Expect

The IRCC benchmark stands at 5.57% as of October 1. But that does not mean your interest rate will be exactly that. Banks add their own lending margin. Depending on your financial profile, you could end up with a 7-8% variable rate. Alternatively, you can lock in a fixed rate, which currently sits at a similar level.

Here lies the trade-off. If you choose a variable rate, your monthly installments can rise. If you go with a fixed rate, you pay slightly more upfront, but you get total peace of mind. Most buyers opt for fixed rates in 2026. It is more predictable and far safer.

Pre-Approval: The First Step Toward Your Financing

Before signing anything with the developer, you need to know exactly how much you can borrow. The bank will calculate your debt-to-income ratio, capped at a maximum of 40% of your net monthly income. If you earn 5,000 lei, your monthly payment cannot exceed 2,000 lei.

Getting pre-approved takes between 3 and 7 days. If all your financial documents are in order, the process is swift. However, if you have negative credit marks or irregular income, the bank might hesitate or ask for a co-borrower.

The Down Payment: How Much Cash You Need Upfront

Banks require a minimum down payment of 15% for RON loans or 20% for EUR loans. But that is not all. The developer may also request installment deposits in line with the Nordis Law. However, that amount is now legally capped and deposited into a protected account.

Let's say the apartment costs 490,000 euros. The bank down payment would be 98,000 euros, and the maximum reservation fee for the developer would be 24,500 euros. In total, you would need 122,500 euros in cash, not counting notary fees and bank commissions.

Fixed vs. Variable Interest Rate: What to Choose

In 2026, fixed interest rates are roughly on par with variable rates. But variable rates can rise or fall depending on market conditions. If you worry about rate spikes, lock in a fixed rate. If you want to capitalize on potential future rate cuts, go with a variable rate.

Keep in mind that for an off-plan property, a variable rate carries extra risk. Construction takes time, and interest rates can climb while the building goes up. Unless you have substantial financial flexibility, a fixed rate is the safer path.

The Step-by-Step Process: From Reservation to Handover

  • Reserve the apartment. Pay a maximum of 5% of the purchase price and sign a reservation agreement.
  • Get pre-approved. Submit your financial documents to the bank and await approval.
  • Sign the preliminary sales agreement. The notary registers the agreement in the Land Registry.
  • Pay the staged down payments. According to the construction schedule agreed upon with the developer.
  • Finalize the mortgage loan. Once construction is complete, the bank appraises the property and releases the funds.
  • Receive your keys. And move into your brand-new home.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to get a mortgage for an off-plan apartment?
It depends. Pre-approval takes 3-7 days. However, the final mortgage is only disbursed once construction is finished. You could wait anywhere from 6 months to 2 years, or even longer if the project encounters delays.
Can I switch banks during construction?
Yes, but it is complicated. You must find another bank willing to take over the financing and pay early repayment fees. If market rates drop significantly, it might be worth it. Otherwise, staying with the original bank is usually best.
What happens if the developer goes bankrupt?
The Nordis Law protects you. Your funds are kept in a dedicated account and can be recovered. However, the legal process can be lengthy and stressful. That is why choosing developers with a proven track record is essential.

Three things to remember

  1. 01

    The Nordis Law provides greater legal protection . Developers can no longer take uncontrolled advance payments, and notaries must register contracts immediately.

  2. 02

    Fixed interest rates offer more safety in 2026 . Even if variable rates might drop, the risk is too high for a long-term loan on an off-plan build.

  3. 03

    You need substantial cash reserves . The bank down payment and developer reservation deposits add up quickly. Make sure you have enough savings before committing.
